HOW TO READ THESE VALUES

Every graph is generated from saved experiment artifacts.

Accuracy shows overall correct classifications; macro F1 gives every disease grade equal importance; the confusion matrices show which grades are mistaken for each other. These results are research evidence, not clinical performance claims.
